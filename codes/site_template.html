Flood levels are 2-, 5- and 20-year return levels of daily mean discharge (Gumbel fit to observed annual maxima); instantaneous peaks are higher. Models do not know dam operations: forecasts below reservoirs are indicative. GR4J forecasts start from the latest observed discharge. Test-period skill (Oct 2023 onward, ERA5-Land weather) is shown per gauge. Code: numbered scripts in this repository.
